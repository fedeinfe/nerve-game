How to play

  1. Hold anywhere. The ring charges.
  2. Release in the gold to grow the pot. Your chain grows with it.
  3. Release in the green to bank the pot. The chain resets to one.
  4. Let go too early, before the green, and the charge fizzles. That's a crack.
  5. Hold into the red and it overloads. That's a crack too.

The ring has one safe span, marked by the two white ticks. Miss it below or above and you crack — three cracks end the run. Every round the ring turns faster and the span narrows, so the pot you are carrying is worth more and harder to keep.
