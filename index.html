How to play

  1. Hold anywhere. The ring charges.
  2. Release in the green to bank it safely.
  3. Release in the gold for triple, and your chain grows.
  4. Hold into the red and it overloads. That's a crack.

Three cracks and the run ends. Every bank makes the ring faster and the gold thinner. Banking green is safe — but it resets your chain.
